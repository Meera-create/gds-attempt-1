Vaccination scheme

The NHS provides a free Vaccination scheme for those over 65

If you wish to access this service or find out if you are eligible, select the button below.

You can use the service if you meet all these criteria:

  1. You are over 65 years old
  2. You are a resident in the UK
  3. You are registered to an NHS GP
  4. You have not already had this vaccine in 2026

Before you start

You can also register by post.

The online service is also available in Welsh (ar gael yn Gymraeg).

You can’t register for this service if you’re in the UK illegally.
