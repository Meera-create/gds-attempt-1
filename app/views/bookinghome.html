Use this service to book a free flu vaccination at your local clinic if you are over 65

You can use this service to:

  • Book your appointment or for someone else
  • Check the details of your booking
  • Modify or cancel your booking

Who can use this service?

You can use this service if you are 65 or over. You must also be a UK citizen or permanent resident in the UK.

Before you start:

You’ll need the name of your local health clinic and the date you’d like to get your vaccination.
